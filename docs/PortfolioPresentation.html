Project 03 · Capstone

TRI Multi-Channel Attribution Analysis

Year-long capstone project with the Trauma Resource Institute — using GA4, BigQuery, and Markov Chain models to identify which digital channels drive conversions.

GA4 BigQuery Markov Chain Shapley Values R
Approach
Markov Chain attribution, Shapley value fairness checks, path length analysis
Finding
All 4 channels contribute equally (~20–22 credits each); longer journeys convert better
Recommendation
Use Markov as primary attribution model; invest across all channels, not just last-touch

Project 04 · Campaign Strategy

LA Fitness Holiday Buddy Pass Campaign

Developed a mock holiday campaign for LA Fitness targeting existing members with a seasonal deal centered around a buddy pass — blending retention and referral strategy.

Campaign Design Retention Marketing Referral Strategy
Objective
Re-engage existing members during the holiday season and drive referrals through a buddy pass promotion
Strategy
Holiday urgency + social proof + dual incentive (member reward + friend access)
Key Takeaway
Retention campaigns with referral hooks outperform pure acquisition messaging

Project 05 · Social Media Strategy

Brand Humor in Crisis — Where's the Line?

Strategic analysis inspired by a real article exploring when brands can use humor publicly and when a crisis demands a more measured, serious response.

Social Media Strategy Brand Voice Crisis Comms
Trigger
Article examining the appropriateness of humor when a brand faces public criticism
Framework
Mapped brand scenarios across a humor-appropriateness spectrum with decision criteria
Skills Applied
Social strategy, brand tone analysis, crisis communication frameworks
Key Takeaway
Humor builds brand equity — but context, timing, and audience trust determine when it's a risk
